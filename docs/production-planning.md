# Production planning and workshop queue\n\nGroup 12 adds a workshop-planning layer without creating a second fulfillment system.\n\n## Authority and lifecycle\n\nOrder status remains authoritative for the operational order state. InventoryMovement remains authoritative for finished-goods stock, and RawMaterialMovement remains authoritative for raw-material stock. ProductionJob stores planning metadata: priority, assignee/team, planned start, due date, planned minutes, notes, and a synchronized production-stage mirror.\n\nProduction jobs follow Queued → In Progress → Quality Check → Completed. The matching order flow is Confirmed → In Production → Quality Check → Ready, after which Group 11 delivery takes over. Cancelled production jobs are terminal.\n\nProduction-requiring paid orders cannot skip directly from Confirmed to Ready. Quality Check may return to In Production for rework.\n\n## Automatic job creation\n\nA ProductionJob is created when payment succeeds and the order actually requires workshop work. Custom bouquets always require production. Catalog orders require production when at least one OrderItem has productionQuantity greater than zero. Ready-stock-only orders do not receive production jobs.\n\nThe same synchronization is used by Stripe payment success, admin-confirmed payment, and the Production page's Sync paid orders action.\n\n## Exact stock versus production allocation\n\nGroup 12 adds reservedStockQuantity and productionQuantity to OrderItem.\n\nFor READY_STOCK, the entire order line comes from finished stock. For MADE_TO_ORDER, the entire line becomes production demand. For BOTH, the line can split between available finished stock and workshop production.\n\nExample: if 5 units are ordered and only 2 are available as finished stock, reservedStockQuantity is 2 and productionQuantity is 3.\n\nFor older orders created before Group 12, queue synchronization reconstructs these values from fulfillment mode and historical reservation movements. This makes the deferred migration safer for existing data.\n\n## Material planning\n\nStandard production demand is BOM quantity per SKU multiplied by OrderItem.productionQuantity. Both MADE_TO_ORDER and the production portion of BOTH now consume BOM materials.\n\nA production line is blocked if its variant is missing, its BOM is missing, or current raw-material availability cannot cover the required quantity.\n\nCustom bouquets continue to use their frozen material plan. Because their materials are reserved during payment, the production board reports them as Reserved until production consumes them. A missing expected custom reservation is reported as Blocked.\n\n## Queue material simulation\n\nThe board evaluates active jobs in stage, priority, due-date, and creation order. Priority order is Urgent, High, Normal, Low.\n\nCatalog production begins with available raw stock equal to stockOnHand minus stockReserved. Custom-bouquet reservations are therefore already excluded. When a queued catalog job is fully coverable, its requirements are subtracted from the simulated balance before evaluating later jobs.\n\nThis simulation does not mutate inventory. Actual material consumption remains transactional when Start Production is executed, so the server-side stock checks are still the final race-condition protection.\n\n## Due dates and workload\n\nInitial due dates are derived from the longest configured maximum lead time among production work. Catalog jobs use ProductVariant.leadTimeMaxDays and custom jobs use CustomBouquetRequest.leadTimeMaxDays. If no lead time exists, no due date is invented.\n\nAdmins can edit priority, assignee/team name, planned minutes, planned start date, due date, and workshop notes.\n\nThe workload summary groups active jobs by assignee and shows job count, total planned minutes, urgent count, and overdue count. Unassigned work appears separately.\n\nAn active job is overdue when its due date has passed. Completed and cancelled jobs are never overdue.\n\n## Ready for delivery\n\nWhen production reaches Ready, ProductionJob becomes Completed and Order.fulfillmentStatus remains Ready. The production board exposes a Ready for delivery metric; shipping and delivery remain in the Group 11 workflow.\n\n## Custom bouquet control\n\nAfter payment, custom-bouquet production is controlled through the Production page. The custom-request page no longer provides an independent path to start or complete production, which prevents Quality Check from being bypassed. Custom Completed remains controlled by successful delivery.\n\n## Admin route\n\nThe main workshop route is /admin/production.\n\nIt includes queue metrics, ready-to-start count, blocked-material count, overdue count, ready-for-delivery count, workload by assignee, material shortages, planning fields, production stage actions, and recent completed work.\n\nOrder detail also shows the production plan and each line's finished-stock versus production allocation.\n\n## Migration\n\nMigration remains deferred as requested. The consolidated migration later must include ProductionJob, ProductionPriority, ProductionJobStatus, Order.productionJob, OrderItem.reservedStockQuantity, and OrderItem.productionQuantity. The final migration/typecheck/build pass should validate all accumulated schema changes together.\n
