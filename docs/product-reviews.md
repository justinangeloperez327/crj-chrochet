# Product reviews

Customers can submit a review from `/account/orders` for their own delivered, paid purchase. Each order item can be reviewed once. Ratings range from one to five; review text is limited to 10–1,200 characters and optional titles to 100.

New reviews await moderation in `/admin/reviews`. Approved reviews appear on product pages; unapproved content does not appear publicly. Reviews preserve their association with the purchased order item and cannot be created for someone else's purchase.
