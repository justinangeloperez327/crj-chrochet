# Discounts and promotions

Use `/admin/discounts` to manage codes and automatic offers. Promotions support percentage or fixed values, schedules, product or collection scope, minimum spend, customer eligibility, total redemption limits and per-customer limits.

The server calculates eligible products and savings from stored prices. A checkout reserves the discount's usage allowance; payment consumes that allowance and cancellation or expiry releases it. Customer-submitted totals are never authoritative.

Review the final order summary before publishing an offer. Refunds do not automatically make a previously redeemed promotion available again.
