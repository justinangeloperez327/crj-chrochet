# Customer management

Open `/admin/customers` to search customers and review their orders, addresses, custom bouquet requests and service notes. Admin notes are internal; customers do not see them in their account.

A customer profile is separate from a login account. Guest checkout may create a profile, but historical guest data is linked to an account only after email ownership is verified. A guest using a registered customer's email cannot modify that customer's profile or addresses.

Customers use `/account` to edit their own profile and `/account/addresses` to manage delivery addresses. Account queries are scoped to the signed-in customer's identity.
